Algorithmic Trading Resources

Knowledge

  • Yahoo Help: “What is the adjusted close?”Primary source for how Yahoo builds adj_close: CRSP-style split and dividend multipliers with a worked example. Use for: anything about adjustment.
  • Ian Gow: “Yahoo returns” noteCompares Yahoo returns with CRSP and describes Yahoo's two closes (split-adjusted close, split-and-dividend adjusted). Use for: data quality of free sources.
  • investor.gov: ex-dividend datesSEC investor-education page on ex-dividend and record dates. Use for: when a dividend lands in the bar data.
  • William F. Sharpe, “The Sharpe Ratio” (1994)The author's own definition: excess return over a benchmark per unit of its volatility, ex ante vs ex post. Use for: Sharpe ratio, choosing a benchmark.
  • Andrew Lo, “The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios” (2002)Shows when √time annualization fails (serial correlation) and how to estimate Sharpe's error. Use for: annualizing, significance of backtest Sharpe ratios.

Display formula, then inline math \(\sqrt{252} \approx 15.87\) in running text:

\[ \text{CAGR} = \left(\frac{P_\text{end}}{P_\text{start}}\right)^{1/\text{years}} - 1 \]

The √252 rule assumes days are independent. When returns are autocorrelated it is wrong, sometimes badly.

Drawdowns are asymmetric. After falling 55% you need +123% to get back to the peak (1 / 0.45 − 1).

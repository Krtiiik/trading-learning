# Mission: Algorithmic trading research

> Draft inferred from the repository (README, ARCHITECTURE). Not yet confirmed by the learner.

## Why
Build a personal research workbench and use it to learn algorithmic trading well enough to design, backtest and judge strategies on real ETF data, rather than trusting numbers I don't understand.

## Success looks like
- Can explain every column and statistic the workbench produces, and compute it by hand.
- Can tell when a backtest result is an artefact of the data (adjustment, survivorship, look-ahead) rather than a real edge.
- Can implement and evaluate a simple strategy on the stored universe end to end.

## Constraints
- Learning happens alongside building the `trading` project in Python, pandas and marimo.
- Free data only (Yahoo via yfinance) for now.

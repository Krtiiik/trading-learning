The scout concluded that machine learning and reinforcement learning failed and that only risk management looked real. That was one night, one asset class, daily bars, about 5,000 days of 25 ETFs. None of it is established for crypto, where there are more assets, hourly bars, different participants and derivatives data.

Do not let it narrow the search. "A regularised model on candidate predictors beats the simple rule" and "volatility targeting improves a crypto portfolio" are both open and should be registered and tested like anything else. Its process lessons (costs, selection, post-hoc rules, short holdouts) have their own notes and deserve more trust than its findings.

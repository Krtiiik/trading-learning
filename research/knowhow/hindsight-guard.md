Dev (2023-2024) and most of test (2025 to mid-2026) lie inside what the language models working here have read about. That knowledge cannot be removed, only kept out of the reasoning that selects hypotheses.

Rules: a motivation cites published work or train-period evidence, with the source's sample end date; a paper whose sample runs past 2022 is flagged. Parameters come from the source or a stated convention. "It is well known that X worked" is not a source. The Skeptic checks every supported finding for this. The final report gives test results from July 2026 on as a separate line, because those months are the only ones no model here has background knowledge of.

Honest limit: this reduces the risk and does not remove it. A model's sense of which ideas are "plausible" is itself shaped by later years.

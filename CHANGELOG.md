## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Concept page: valuation, market value and earnings, the P/E ratio with a worked example, why a high P/E can be fair or risky, index P/E, earnings reports and gaps, and why a low P/E is not a buy signal.
- Concept page: compounding, gains that earn gains with the $10,000 at 7% example, the rule of 72, reinvested dividends and accumulating funds, how fees and losses compound, and inflation.
- Lesson: planning one trade, from the written rule through an ATR-based stop, position size, target and costs to a look back at the result.
- Lesson: does this rule work, turning an idea into exact rules, running the 10-trade backtest, reading win rate, expectancy and drawdown, checking for overfitting, look-ahead bias and missing costs, and testing live with small risk.
- Lesson: what your first order really costs, checking the broker in the ČNB register, where an EU broker sends the order, reading the order book, market and limit orders, why evening trading on a German venue costs more, and a worked round trip for a CZK account.
- Lesson: building a simple long-term portfolio, why one broad fund, a 60/40 target mix, choosing a UCITS fund as an EU buyer (TER, accumulating or distributing, domicile, currency), Czech tax on funds, what fees do over 30 years, rebalancing, and living through a 50% fall.
- Concept page: taxes on trading, realised gains, holding periods, allowances, dividend and interest tax, tax-advantaged accounts, a US, UK and Czech comparison (including the 2026 Czech 3-year test and 100,000 CZK limit), and a worked example for each country.
- Concept page: CFD, how a contract for difference works, the broker as the other side, a worked 5:1 stock CFD, going short, costs, the EU (ESMA and ČNB) protections, the appropriateness test, and the "professional client" trap.
- Concept page: currency risk, what a dollar result means for a reader whose money is in koruna or euros, why a fund's holdings and not its listing currency decide the risk, currency-hedged funds, and months versus decades.
- Concept page: rebalancing, target mix and drift, calendar and threshold rebalancing, rebalancing with new money, a worked 60/40 example, and its costs.
- Concept page: volume profile, volume at each price, the point of control, the value area, high- and low-volume nodes, and a worked profile of the $45–$55 stock.
- Concept page: REIT (real estate investment trust), equity and mortgage REITs, the payout rule, FFO, a worked dividend yield example, why rising rates hurt REIT prices, and US tax on REIT dividends. The REIT summary now covers property loans too.
- Concept page: buy and hold, compounding with reinvested dividends, how fees compound, time in the market, why a broad index fund, and long drawdowns.
- Concept page: momentum, ranking many assets by past return, the 12-month look-back that skips the latest month, a worked monthly re-rank with rebalancing, and momentum crashes. The momentum summary now says the top risers are expected to keep beating the rest.
- Concept page: MACD, the exponential moving average, the MACD and signal lines, the histogram, signal-line and zero-line crossovers, and why its values cannot be compared across stocks.
- Concept page: chart pattern, double tops and bottoms, head and shoulders, triangles and flags, the neckline, the measured-move target, and a worked double top.
- Concept page: option, calls and puts, strike, premium, exercise and assignment, intrinsic and time value, time decay, a worked call on the $42 stock, and the risk for buyers and sellers.
- Concept page: commodity, the three groups, spot and futures prices, cost of carry, contango and backwardation, the roll cost of futures funds, and the ways to trade commodities.
- Concept page: futures, standard contracts with an expiry date, the multiplier, initial and maintenance margin, daily settlement, expiry and rolling, and why you can lose more than your margin.
- Concept page: breakout trading, the entry, stop and target rules, a worked trade with position sizing, why the win rate is often low, and why chasing a breakout spoils the risk/reward.
- Concept page: RSI (relative strength index), how it compares average gains with average losses, the 70 and 30 levels, why strong trends hold it at an extreme, and divergence.
- Concept page: bond, face value, coupon and maturity, credit risk and ratings, why prices fall when interest rates rise, and why longer bonds carry more interest rate risk.
- Concept page: dividend, the four dates and why you must buy before the ex-date, why the price drops on the ex-date, dividend yield, and US withholding tax for holders outside the US.
- Concept page: breakout, what counts as a breakout (a close beyond the zone), volume as a rule of thumb, retests, false breakouts and downward breakouts.
- Concept page: ETF, how an exchange-traded fund tracks an index, why its price stays close to its holdings, the expense ratio, kinds of ETF, which ETFs private traders in the EU and UK can buy, and leveraged and inverse ETFs.
- Concept page: short selling, the steps (borrow, sell, buy back, return), the borrow fee and dividends, US rules, other ways to go short, and why the possible loss has no limit.
- Concept page: Bollinger bands, the standard deviation bands around a moving average, the usual setting, the squeeze, and why a touch of a band is not a signal by itself.
- Concept page: diversification, company-specific and market-wide risk, correlation, and why correlated positions add up to one larger bet.
- Concept page: support and resistance, levels where falls or rises stopped more than once, levels as zones, why they can work, role reversal, and placing a stop and a target around them.
- Concept page: candlestick pattern, common one- to three-candle patterns (doji, hammer, shooting star, engulfing, morning star), why context and confirmation matter, and why a pattern alone is a weak signal.
- Concept page: entry and exit rules, what makes a rule exact (condition, time, order), the kinds of exit, and turning a vague idea into exact rules.
- Concept page: mean reversion, buying a price far below its usual level and selling when it comes back, how far is measured, and why it has many small wins and a few large losses.
- Concept page: index, what an index tracks, major indices by market, cap-weighted and price-weighted indices, points and percent, and how people trade an index.
- Concept page: peak and trough, how to spot turning points (swing highs and lows), why they are confirmed late, and how they place stops and targets.
- Concept page: line vs candle chart, the same prices drawn two ways, what a line hides (swings and gaps), and bar charts.
- Concept page: ATR (average true range), true range on a gap day, Wilder's average, and using ATR to set the stop and the position size.
- Concept page: risk/reward, the possible gain on a trade compared with the possible loss, and the win rate a ratio needs to break even.
- Concept page: broker, what a broker does for you, the two kinds of broker, and the protection schemes that pay clients back when a broker fails (US, UK, EU).
- Concept page: leverage, the leverage ratio, margin, margin calls and stop outs, and the leverage limits for private traders in the EU, UK and US.
- Concept page: cryptocurrency, what a coin and a blockchain are, keys and wallets, stablecoins, crypto pairs, and who holds your coins on an exchange.
- Concept page: volatility, how big a price's moves are (not their direction), how traders measure it, and how it sets the stop, the position size and the target.
- Concept page: drawdown, how far an account has fallen from its high, maximum drawdown, time under water, and the larger gain needed to recover.

### Changed

- Lessons now follow a reading path: the four beginner lessons have a step number, show first on the home page and the Lessons page, and each one ends with a link to the next lesson. The Lessons shelf moved to the top of the home page. The portfolio lesson stays separate, for long-term investors.
- Catalog: put Risk and money in teaching order (volatility, stop loss, position sizing, risk/reward, then results), put entry and exit rules right after strategy, and move CFD and dividend to Assets.
- Catalog: plan five new concepts: valuation, slippage, trading journal, compounding and trading psychology.
- The stop loss summary now fits short positions too: it closes a position once the price reaches your level.
- The portfolio lesson says "expense ratio (in Europe, TER)" like the ETF page.
- The home page and group pages no longer show a written count and progress meter per group. The home page and footer show only the total number of concepts and lessons.
- Link hover previews show the linked page's banner above its title and summary, like the cards. A preview that does not fit below the link opens above it.
- The theme switch is one button that cycles System, Light and Dark, in place of three buttons. On phones it now stays in the top row next to search.
- Pages no longer follow a concept link with a parenthesis that repeats the link's hover preview; where the parenthesis also gave an example value or other detail, only that detail stays.
- Pages agree after the fifth site-wide audit: exchange now says MiCA makes EU crypto firms keep coins apart, CFD and leverage agree on who sets the EU CFD rules, the shared trade treats the $54 target as a check on risk-reward and entry-and-exit rules, the portfolio lesson links the ETF page instead of repeating it, REIT gives the Czech dividend tax, and the new lessons are linked from broker, order types, ETF, diversification and currency risk.
- EU and Czech notes on 23 concept pages: the Czech compensation fund and ČNB register (broker), Xetra, Euronext and Prague exchanges and CET times (exchange, trading hours), ESMA and ČNB CFD rules (leverage), the EU short selling rules, Czech dividend tax, accumulating and distributing funds, domicile and TER (ETF), currency notes (stock, bond, dividend, commodity, forex pair, diversification, buy and hold), the Czech 3-year test (momentum, rebalancing, buy and hold), MiCA (cryptocurrency), Eurex DAX futures, EU warrants and turbos (option), and links to the new CFD, currency risk and taxes pages.
- Learning pages aligned after the fourth site-wide audit: REIT now has incoming links, the volume profile explains why the edges of the $45–$55 stock are thin, older pages link the new owners (chart pattern, MACD, momentum, commodity, rebalancing), glosses and terms match their owners, and chart pattern now follows candlestick pattern in the catalog.
- Breakout trading: the stop explanation now compares the 80-cent distance below the zone, not the $2 risk, with one ATR.
- Learning pages agree with each other after a third site-wide audit: each page that sets a stop on the shared $45–$55 stock says how the stop was set and how it differs from the others, "trading range", "zone" and "band" each have one meaning, the ETF, gap and ATR glosses are the same everywhere, dividend pages say "dividend yield", and the fees page lists the ETF expense ratio and the borrow fee.
- Learning pages agree with each other after a second site-wide audit: CFDs are explained the same way and owned by the broker page, the drawdown page alone gives the recovery maths, the calm and jumpy stocks are described by their ATR, Bitcoin's volatility is worded the same on every page, an account's highest value is its "high" (not a "peak"), and the candle chart lesson now says a candle's colour compares its close with its own open.
- The repository holds only the learning site and is named `trading-learning`. The site moves to <https://krtiiik.github.io/trading-learning/>; the old `/trading/` address stops working.
- Site sources are at the repository root. Build with `uv run python scripts/build.py` (output in `dist/`), preview with `uv run python scripts/serve.py --open`, test with `uv run pytest`.
- Concept pages agree with each other after a site-wide audit: "position size" means the number of shares (its money value is the position value), payment for order flow and slippage are named where they are described, US-only rules on the exchange page say so, and bid, ask, spread and colour wording is the same on every page.
